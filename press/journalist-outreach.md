## Sending mechanics

- **X DMs** to public-figure accounts may bounce if they don't follow you back. If bounce, reply to their most recent on-topic tweet with a one-line + link instead.
- **Email** is higher conversion for first contact than DM for journalists at major outlets.
- **No mass-send.** Send one per day for 10 days. Track replies in a sheet.
- **Subject lines that work:** "Re: [their article title]" or "Quick tool for [specific verification challenge they mentioned]"

## What I won't auto-do (and why)

- ❌ Auto-send these cold emails / DMs. Cold outreach has 100x conversion when it's clearly *you* responding to their actual recent work. Auto-sent template emails get reported and burn the sending domain.
- ❌ Scrape their email addresses from gated sources. Listed addresses above are public masthead emails. Verify before sending.
- ❌ Mass-add to a "press list" mailing system. The whole game here is one-to-one.

Send 2-3 per day from your Gmail. Track responses. The ones that bite get free Max accounts and a demo Zoom.
